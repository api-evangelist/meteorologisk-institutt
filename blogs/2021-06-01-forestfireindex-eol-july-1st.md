Some product changes, mainly of interest to Norwegian users: Forestfireindex This product will be terminated on July 1st, as it has been replaced by MetAlerts on the new Yr website. We are working on a new map-based service, which is currently in beta and available on skogbrannfare.met.no . The old retro.yr.no site will link to this after July 1st.
