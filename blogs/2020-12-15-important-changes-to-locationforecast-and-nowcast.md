After several months in beta, Nowcast 2.0 is finally officially launched. Both this and Locationforecast have now got a definite date for termination of the old versions. Continuation of XML format Based on popular feedback we have decided to continue the XML format for Locationforecast and Nowcast into the foreseeable future.
