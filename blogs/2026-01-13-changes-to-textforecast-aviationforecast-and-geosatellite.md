textforecast/2.0 EOL on 2025-03-16 Version 3.0 has been out of beta since March last year, and it's time to retire the old version. It will expire on 16 March, so please update your systems to handle the new GeoJSON format instead of the old proprietary XML format. As previously notified, the landoverview forecast period is now only two days, instead of three as in earlier versions.
