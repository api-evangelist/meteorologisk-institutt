We will shortly be making some changes to the existing versions of Textforecast and Locationforecast. Textforecast The seahigh and seabank products will be replaced with a new, auto-generated product (named sea ) which will be available on December 3rd (possibly earlier). The old forecasts will continue production until February 1st, after which requests will be returned the new sea forecast instead.
