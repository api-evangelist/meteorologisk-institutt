Unfortunately, api.met.no (along with most other services from MET Norway) was unavailable yesterday 16 Feb between 15 and 16 UTC, due to a loss of Internet connection at our Oslo office. Apparently this was caused by some scheduled router maintenance at our ISP which failed, and the automatic failover to our other line didn't work as expected. Sorry about that.
