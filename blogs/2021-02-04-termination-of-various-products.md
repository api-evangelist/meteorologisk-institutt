All good things must come to an end, hopefully to be replaced by something better. Here is an update of forthcoming changes to the weather APIs. Locationforecast and Nowcast As mentioned previously, the old versions of Locationforecast (1.9), Nowcast (0.9) and Weathericon (1.1) will terminate on March 1st, 2021.
