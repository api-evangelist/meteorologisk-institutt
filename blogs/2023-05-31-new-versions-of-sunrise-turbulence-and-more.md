While it's almost been six months since the last update, we have been quite busy and have several important announcements to make before the summer break. Sunrise 3.0 The venerable Sunrise version 2.0, which is our second most popular product, is being retired. Consisting of 100K lines of Fortran code, it has become impossible to maintain after the main developer left us, and is being replaced with a much smaller Python library which uses GeoJSON as its standard output format instead of XML.
